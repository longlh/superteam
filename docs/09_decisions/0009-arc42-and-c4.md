# ADR 9. The documents follow arc42, and the structure follows C4

- Status: Accepted

## Context

The documents of the structure, of the flows, and of the requirements were mixed
in the same files, and the same flow was written in four places.

## Decision

The documents follow the 12 chapters of arc42, each one a folder named
`NN_name`. The building blocks follow the C4 model. The documents of the
structure do not hold a flow: the flows are in `06_runtime/`, drawn as sequence
diagrams. The requirements and the use cases are in `01_introduction/`.

## Consequences

- A chapter that has no content yet is an empty folder with a `.gitkeep`.
- The known issues and the open questions are gathered in `11_risks-and-debts/`.
- `docs/draft/` is legacy, and it is not migrated.
